MASHOOD AHMAD — EDITOR

Mashood Ahmad is a video editor focused on short-form and social content — the kind that has to earn attention in the first second. His edits lean on clean cuts, purposeful transitions, and motion graphics that support the story instead of distracting from it.

Trained through a structured path across Premiere Pro, CapCut, Alight Motion, and InShot, he works comfortably across both desktop and mobile-first editing workflows — a rare combination that fits how modern content actually gets made and published.

Currently taking on freelance projects: reels, YouTube edits, motion titles, and color grading.
